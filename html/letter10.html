Letter 10

Jinny Lee? Korean, bangs, long hair, pretty eyes? Yeahh of course I know her she’s my girlfriend! Ah she is brilliant. The best girlfriend in the world and I say this without bias. I still can’t believe I got to meet someone like her and she loves me back???? Me??? Oh man I just know if she hears me saying this she’s going to say something like self-deprecating or whatnot but what can I say but the truth? You’ve got to meet her properly; she’ll love your cats. Don’t get me even started on cats. She’s crazy about cats. Never even owned one! But she feeds the stray cats around her house and pets the ones in the street. I even dreamt about petting a cat yesterday and I thought about taking a photo to send to Jinny. Heck, I’m scared she might leave me for another man who’s not terribly allergic to cats! I’m kidding of course; she’s one of the most loyal and patient people there is. So many times I made mistakes and fricked up and every time we talked about it and figured it out. The maturity of a grandma, eyesight of a middle-aged woman. Can you believe that she’s not even 20? Well, she certainly doesn’t look like it (and doesn’t act like one either ㅎ)

How long has it been? Exactly… … days now! I’ve told her a couple times that it feels like we’ve known each other for a really long time but also time has gone so fast. I swear time is passing faster and faster and I can attribute a pretty big portion of that to Jinny. Whenever we meet, the entire day just blinks by. It’s so annoying. But also it means that I’m having the time of my life which is good too. I’m more excited about growing up and making money and settling down because she came into my life. Like a life goal. It’s not like I have no goals outside her and I am willing to die once I reach my goals with her, but she fills my life with a lot of fun and purpose. Isn’t that pretty great??

Sometimes I feel like I’m a bit incompetent around her. Especially when I try to help her or do something for her and I mess up, knowing that if it was the opposite, she would do such a better job. I don’t think I could ever be cocky around her. It just wouldn’t be physically possible. But I’m glad this is the case. I can improve that much as a person and get better at cooking, gifting, talking, and being a nice person in general. It’s just a win win for me.

Next time you see her, you should say hi! But tell her cats are super bad for those with allergies. Direct her instead to a dog shelter or something. I don’t want to die ! I’m gonna go home now and maybe buy some bread along the way. I think she said she wanted a baguette. I bet she’s taking a nap on the beanbag. I’m gonna try taking a nap right next to her so she can wake up to me napping and not feel as guilty. See you!

Phillip
