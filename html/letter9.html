Letter 9

Heyyyyyy thanks for doing that little gameee honestly thanks for even going into this website and doing this even though you must be insanely busy right now. I’m always grateful for every second you decide to spend with me. You could be studying, watching webtoon, clicking on ducks, napping, socializing, making tea, anything else. But you decide to spend it with me. And I could not be more honored and happy.


You really make me feel like your top priority and that makes me so happy and reassured. The password for this letter is “blessed” because I truly think you are. We were going around in a circle during church yesterday and everyone would say which gift each of us received from God. Apparently I’m wise? Chill? Strategic? I honestly forgot. But I think you are blessed with so many gifts. Leadership. Empathy. Love. You are my miracle and there is not a day when I’m not thankful for your existence in my life. And your mum’s. Your uncle’s. 아린이랑 시안이’s too. It’ll be a tough few days and I totally get your feeling of being completely lost and powerless. But aren’t we all? Anything could happen anytime and it could completely change our lives. But we trust in God and hope that He’ll figure it out for us. As He’s done all this time. When it’s getting tough, text me. You’re very very special to me.
